Privacy Policy for CabCalc

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use CabCalc, we collect the following information:

  • Account Data: Your email address and password for authentication and account management.
  • Profile Data: Your name and phone number (if provided in the app settings).
  • App Data: Shift records, revenue, total trips, hired kilometers, tolls, normal/online/airport deductions, and commission slabs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data solely to provide and improve our service. Specifically, to calculate your net income, daily commission, and sync your data securely across your devices.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (Firestore Database and Authentication) to securely store and sync your data in the cloud. Firebase has its own strict privacy and security policies.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties, marketing agencies, or advertisers.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase infrastructure. We implement standard database security rules to ensure that only you (the authenticated user) can access, read, or modify your own data.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, you can contact the developer through the app's feedback section.
